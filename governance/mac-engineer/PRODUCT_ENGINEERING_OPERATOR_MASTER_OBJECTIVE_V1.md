## PRIMARY CHARACTER

The target system is:

- local-first;
- Mac-native where appropriate;
- evidence-driven;
- fail-closed;
- repeatable;
- recoverable;
- repository-aware;
- institutional-memory-aware;
- capable of reading current technical truth before mutation;
- capable of turning human intent into bounded engineering objectives;
- capable of building, testing, repairing and verifying real products;
- capable of producing durable Evidence;
- capable of stopping at Human Threshold when human authority is required;
- capable of returning a verified result rather than merely generating code.
